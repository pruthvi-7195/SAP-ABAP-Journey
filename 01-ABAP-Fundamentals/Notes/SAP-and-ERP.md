# SAP and ERP

## What is SAP?

SAP stands for Systems, Applications, and Products in Data Processing.

SAP provides enterprise software used to manage and integrate business
processes across an organization.

## What is ERP?

ERP stands for Enterprise Resource Planning.

ERP integrates different business functions into a centralized system.

## Common SAP Modules

- FI — Financial Accounting
- CO — Controlling
- MM — Materials Management
- SD — Sales and Distribution
- PP — Production Planning
- HCM — Human Capital Management
- QM — Quality Management
- PM — Plant Maintenance

## Interview Definition

SAP is an enterprise software platform used to integrate and manage
business processes such as finance, sales, procurement, inventory
and production.
