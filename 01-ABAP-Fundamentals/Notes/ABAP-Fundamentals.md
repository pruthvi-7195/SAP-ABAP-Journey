# ABAP Fundamentals

## What is ABAP?

ABAP stands for Advanced Business Application Programming.

It is SAP's programming language used to develop, customize and
extend SAP applications.

## Why is ABAP Used?

ABAP is used for:

- Reports
- Business logic
- Interfaces
- Enhancements
- Forms
- Data processing
- Custom applications
- Integration with SAP systems
